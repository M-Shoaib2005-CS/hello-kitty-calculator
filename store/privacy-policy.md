# Catularor Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

Catularor ("the app") is an offline maths app. This policy explains what it does with information.

**We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.**

## What stays on your device
The app saves your settings (night mode, sound, vibration, calm mode), your recent calculator history, quest progress (stars, streak, hat), and saved formulas in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. If you choose Profile → Backup, the app shows a text code that you can copy yourself; the app never sends it anywhere. You can erase the saved data any time with Profile → Reset, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## What the app does not do
- No account or sign-in
- No advertising and no ad SDKs
- No analytics or crash-reporting services
- No in-app purchases
- No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files
- No internet connection is needed or used

## Children
The app is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing to collect from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
